Coding Art Fundamentals · Policies

Policies

Just the two policies that matter here: attendance, and how AI tools fit into this course.

Attendance

Three or more unexcused absences result in a No Credit for the course. Students should sign in at every class. Classes are recorded, so a student who misses one can catch up on the content, though this does not substitute for attendance credit.

Statement on Artificial Intelligence

Students are asked not to use AI tools such as ChatGPT, Copilot, or Claude to complete their weekly assignments, particularly in the first half of the semester, while core skills are still being built by hand. The reasoning is straightforward: without understanding what the underlying concept is actually doing, a student cannot tell when an AI tool’s suggestion is wrong, or adapt it to their own work. This course builds that understanding first, one concept at a time, so that later, when AI tools do assist, students can read and verify what is produced rather than trusting it blindly.

A required workshop on using AI tools well takes place in Week 14, once the fundamentals are solid enough for AI to help rather than become a crutch.
