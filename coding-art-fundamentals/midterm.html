Coding Art Fundamentals · Midterm

Midterm Project

Due Week 7 — each student builds their own challenge, pulling together everything from the first half of the course.

What it is
Each student creates their own challenge: developing a story, building something they personally resonate with, at any level. The goal is to create something, simple or not, that the student is proud of and enjoys, built in a way that requires solving their own problems to reach their own goal.
Leading up to it
Week 6 (“Classes”) is pre-midterm consolidation — no separate assignment that week, since the material is covered by the midterm instead.
Covers
Everything from Weeks 1 through 6, including the custom class from Week 6: variables, conditionals, loops, randomness, arrays, a custom function, and a custom class.
Format
An interactive p5.js sketch.
Scheduling
Week 7 (Oct 21). No fixed time slot; scheduling is coordinated across all three Fall 2026 Creative Computing courses.
Weight
20% of your final grade. See Grading for the full breakdown.
