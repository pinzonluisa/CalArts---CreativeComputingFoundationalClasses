Creative Computing

Coding Music Fundamentals, Coding Art Fundamentals, and Intro to TouchDesigner are three separate courses taught in the same semester, each meeting on its own weekly schedule. Each course covers its own material and has its own weekly work. They share a common calendar, and each course’s final project must connect to at least one of the other two.

Credit Load

All three courses are 3 credits, and follow CalArts’ standard credit-hour policy: 45 hours of work per credit for BFA students, 60 hours per credit for Masters students. That’s 135 total hours for BFA students and 180 for Masters students across the semester, per course. On top of class time, that works out to roughly 7.8 hours of independent work a week for BFA students, and roughly 11 hours a week for Masters students. This workload is per course; it doesn’t shrink for a student taking more than one of the three in the same semester.
