Signal Minus Threshold

The top graph is an RMS signal, always between 0 and 1. The bottom graph is that same signal with a Math CHOP subtracting a threshold, no clamping. Whatever was above the threshold line comes out positive, whatever was below it comes out negative. Drag the slider and watch the balance shift.

A point on the signal only turns negative once the threshold is subtracted past it. Drag the slider above and watch this same point cross from positive to negative.

At threshold 0, the whole signal sits at or above the line, almost entirely positive. At threshold 1, almost the whole signal falls below it, negative, except the very tallest peaks.
